# Privacy Policy — Dad's Groceries

_Last updated: 5 July 2026_

## What we collect
Dad's Groceries stores grocery list data (item names, quantities, store names, and optional photos) in a Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Storage instance that you control. No data is collected by the app developer.

## How data is used
Data is used solely to display and synchronise your grocery lists across devices in your household. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

## Data sharing
Your data is stored in your own Firebase project. It is not shared with any third party by the app developer. Google Firebase's own privacy policy applies to data stored in Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Photos
Photos attached to items are uploaded to your Firebase Storage bucket. They are not accessed by the app developer.

## Image search
When you use the Auto-search photo feature, the item name is sent to a Vercel-hosted API route that queries Google Custom Search on your behalf. No personal information is sent. The Google Privacy Policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
